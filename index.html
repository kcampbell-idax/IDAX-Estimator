Import Study area — import KML/KMZ or aerial for staffing estimate

Drop your study area file here after selecting your study type above. KML or KMZ exports from Kapturrit, mymap, or any GIS tool give the most accurate results — the tool reads your polylines (blockfaces) and polygons (lots) directly from the geometry, calculates polygon area using MUTCD guidelines to estimate spaces where no stalls are drawn, and applies the correct coverage rates for your study type. An aerial screenshot also works.
